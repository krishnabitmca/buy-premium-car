# Implementation delivery plan

The owner’s 19 October mandate and [Phase 1 contract](PHASE1_EXECUTION.md)
supersede the feature order below. Acquisition/scan truth, source breadth and
operations precede optional AI and advanced comparison. Retain this older table
as historical scope; see ADR 028 for the initial contract increment.

Agreed order, 18 October 2026. Each increment needs code, acceptance evidence and
an independently verified deployment before it is described as live.

| Order | Work | Current implementation state | Closure gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Measurable coverage | Existing sparse source/location/make/model matrix, declared scopes and renewal capacity; this increment adds exclusion diagnosis and source queue age | Independent eligible-listing benchmark and actionable measured gaps, without fabricated market percentages |
| 2 | Reliable continuous acquisition | Durable continuation, claim fencing, renewal/expiry and fair scheduling exist; this increment preserves operator source governance | Sustained dispatch/backlog recovery, complete-scan contract and safe reconciliation; no failed/partial scan implies sold |
| 3 | Source expansion | Reviewed adapters and incremental dealer/OEM acquisition exist | Expand according to measured gaps, including explicitly verified demo inventory and source-specific traversal evidence |
| 4 | Facts and deal accuracy | Conservative identity and current comparison cohorts exist | Labelled real identity/availability/deal evaluation and conflict review |
| 5 | Public-launch controls | Token/consent and recovery foundations exist | Verified account/destination ownership, safe sessions, recoverable delivery, restore/readiness and controlled delivery pilot |
| 6 | Buyer journeys | Device-local save/share/compare and current-offer revalidation implemented (ADR 024); live Honda City journey checked | Verified account sync, broader buyer testing and controlled watches remain separate |
| 7 | Grounded AI | Consented Gemini intent plus constrained cited evidence selection (ADR 027) | Held-out intent/question relevance, multilingual quality and real buyer evaluation; unrestricted advice is not delivered |
| 8 | Mobile | Public PWA offline shell and network recovery implemented (ADR 026), Chromium tested | Actual device installation and assistive-technology acceptance; native distribution remains separate |
| 9 | National capacity | PostgreSQL search and optional Elasticsearch projection/metadata cache exist | Measured national workloads and India-network latency; incremental projection, query parity, cursors and measured operating cost |
| 10 | Purchase assistance | Destination context exists | Sourced transport/registration/tax assumptions and location-aware cost estimates |

Privacy-conscious analytics and real buyer evaluation run throughout. Remaining
product requirements in BUYER_EXPERIENCE_REQUIREMENTS.md and search targets in
AI_SEARCH_ACCEPTANCE.md remain authoritative. This plan does not mark them closed.

## First increment acceptance

- Routine bootstrap cannot undo a source or endpoint disable, removed capability
  or parser override; explicit reviewed activation still works.
- Latest-observation exclusion reasons match customer search, including missing
  observations and expired/disabled stock.
- Private reports retain disabled-source backlog, queue age and overlapping reason
  semantics; no public route, inventory mutation or paid resource is introduced.
- Unit and disposable PostgreSQL integration checks verify these boundaries.
