# 016: Measure declared coverage and capacity before expanding acquisition

Brand presence is not inventory completeness. The October 18 baseline had results
for 42 of 44 makes but thousands of overdue offers and incomplete city/model
traversals. Several nominally live sources contributed no current inventory.

Add a private operational report with a sparse source × seller-location-label ×
brand/model × condition matrix. Use the actual latest observation and the shared
customer offer-eligibility predicates. Keep excluded historical offers visible
as excluded evidence. Distinguish offer counts from canonical vehicle counts;
vehicles can occur in multiple source/location cells, so these are not additive
national unique-car totals. Seller labels are not normalized cities or buyer
destination boundaries.

Declare expected scopes only from registered roots, actual queue intents and
reviewed advertised catalogue links. A visited city landing page does not prove
that city's pagination is complete. Source-advertised counts can overlap, expire,
or include unavailable cars; never sum them into a national denominator. Keep
market coverage percentages null until an independent eligible-listing benchmark
exists. Missing cells are unknown coverage, not an empty market.

Report per-source actionable gaps: adapter/feed review, source failures, pending
page slots, unknown condition and inconclusive detail parsers. Rank work using
observed backlog, retaining the existing worker claim and origin pacing fences.
Record pages and offers for the same successful batch; cumulative pages cannot
serve as the denominator for latest-batch extraction yield.
Retain that batch's own observation timestamp when a later attempt fails, and
report the latest queue attempt separately from prior traversal evidence.

Capacity scenarios distinguish recurring individual checks from configured
attempt slots, policy-overdue offers from retry-ready jobs, and unknown-condition
or shared-catalogue URLs from individually renewable listings. Recorded pipeline
runs include manual executions and are not proof of hosted scheduler cadence.
An individual-check capacity deficit is not a recommendation to buy that many
servers: bulk catalogue batches, extraction yield, origin limits and dispatch
delays determine actual throughput. No paid resources are provisioned.

The standalone service reserves the same ten-job workload mix as the scheduled
worker: one general, one demo, four catalogue, two model and two continuation
jobs. October 18 catalogue validation yielded 89 and 72 source offers across three
pages each, with substantial remaining city work. This shifts two existing slots
toward bulk acquisition while retaining every work class and the ten-job ceiling.
Catalogue slots execute before discovery fan-out. A failed bucket does not
skip the other buckets or listing renewal. Idle demo/used renewal slots can be
borrowed by the other condition while both retain alternating reservations when
both have ready work. Unclassified offers need catalogue classification evidence
and are no longer enrolled into a queue that cannot claim them.

Every refresh workflow publishes JSON and readable Markdown evidence. The daily
national report includes these operations alongside the existing make summary.
Reports are private workflow/local artifacts; no customer UI, public database
grant, new credential, or historical inventory fallback is introduced.
